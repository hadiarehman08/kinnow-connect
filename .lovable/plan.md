# KinnowLink 60-second vertical demo video

## Deliverable
- One exactly 60-second MP4 at 1080 × 1920, separate from the app.
- A polished portrait product walkthrough using freshly captured KinnowLink screens, not pasted raw recording.
- Natural English narration, synchronized subtitles, restrained background music, tap sounds, and transition effects.

## Visual direction
- Premium agricultural technology style using forest green `#064E3B`, citrus orange `#F97316`, warm ivory `#F8F6EF`, soft sage `#DDE9DD`, and white.
- Manrope display typography with Inter body text.
- A staged mobile interface with smooth reframing, orange touch ripples, thin connector lines, short callouts, soft glows, and subtle depth.
- Subtitles remain inside phone-safe margins and shift away from important interface controls.

## Sequence
- **0:00–0:06:** Animated grower-to-buyer connection, KinnowLink logo, and welcome screen.
- **0:06–0:19:** Grower role selection, dashboard, My Lots, and the real Post a Lot form.
- **0:19–0:32:** Buyer experience, Find Lots search/filtering, a sample lot, and Requirements.
- **0:32–0:46:** Real Matches screen with compatibility reasons highlighted without scores or guarantees.
- **0:46–0:54:** Messages inbox and working conversation view, clearly shown as demo/local activity.
- **0:54–1:00:** Logo close with “List supply,” “Discover demand,” and “Connect through matches.”

## Production approach
- Capture clean portrait screenshots from the current app for every demonstrated state.
- Rebuild the presentation in Remotion with deterministic frame-based motion and consistent transitions.
- Generate and time the supplied narration, then create phrase-level caption timing from the final audio.
- Compose original low-volume ambient electronic music and simple interface sound cues.
- Spot-check key frames for readability, render the MP4, and verify exact duration, dimensions, video/audio tracks, and final file size.

## Accuracy safeguards
- Keep the real KinnowLink logo, labels, navigation, fictional records, and prototype disclaimers.
- Do not invent screens, successful transactions, or real marketplace activity.
- Show only interactions confirmed to work in the current build.
